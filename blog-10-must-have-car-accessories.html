10 Must-Have Car Accessories Every Driver Should Own in 2026

By CarGadgetFinds Editorial Team · Updated October 2026 · 7 min read

Whether you drive a 10-year-old commuter or a brand-new truck, the right accessories make every trip safer, more comfortable, and more enjoyable. We've compiled the ten accessories that deliver the most value for money — most cost under $50 and install in minutes.

1. Dash Cam — The #1 Safety Investment

A dash cam is the single most valuable accessory you can buy. It protects you from false insurance claims, hit-and-run drivers, and unfair police reports. Many insurers offer discounts for dash cam owners. Look for at least 2K resolution, night vision, and loop recording. See our top picks →

2. Backup Camera — Eliminates Blind Spots

If your car was built before 2018, it likely doesn't have a factory backup camera. A $40 wireless model installs in 30 minutes and could save you from backing over a pet, child, or parked car. Essential for trucks, SUVs, and anyone who parks in tight spaces. See our top picks →

3. Tire Inflator & Gauge — Never Get Stranded

A portable 12V tire inflator ($30–$50) plugs into your cigarette lighter and inflates a flat tire in 3–5 minutes. Combined with a digital tire gauge, you'll always have proper tire pressure — which improves gas mileage by 3–5% and prevents blowouts. Check your pressure monthly, especially before long trips.

4. Jumper Cables or Portable Jump Starter

A dead battery always happens at the worst time. Portable jump starters ($50–$100) are about the size of a water bottle, can start your car 20+ times on one charge, and double as a phone charger. They're far more convenient than traditional jumper cables — no need to flag down another driver.

5. Car Cover — Protect Your Paint

If you park outdoors, a quality car cover pays for itself in one season. UV rays fade paint, bird droppings etch the clear coat, and tree sap bonds permanently. A $50–$100 weatherproof cover prevents all of this. Choose one with mirror pockets, a cable lock, and UV-resistant fabric. Browse car covers →

6. Floor Mats & Seat Covers

All-weather floor mats (WeatherTech or similar) catch mud, snow, and spills — protecting your carpet and resale value. For families with kids or pets, seat covers are equally essential. Both are cheap insurance against permanent stains and wear.

7. Emergency Roadside Kit

A basic kit ($30) should include: reflective triangle, first aid supplies, flashlight with extra batteries, multi-tool, duct tape, and a blanket. Add a warm coat and water in winter. You hope to never use it — but you'll be grateful it's there when you need it.

8. Phone Mount & Fast Charger

A magnetic or vent phone mount keeps your phone visible for navigation without holding it in your hand (illegal in many states). Pair it with a USB-C fast charger — modern phones charge from 0–50% in 30 minutes. Wireless charging pads are convenient but slower.

9. Window Deflectors & Sun Shade

Window deflectors let you crack your windows in rain without getting wet — great for hot days when you want ventilation without rain. A windshield sun shade ($15–$25) keeps your interior 30–40°F cooler on hot days, protecting your dash and making the car bearable when you return.

10. Trunk Organizer & Cargo Net

A collapsible trunk organizer ($20) keeps groceries, tools, and emergency gear from rolling around. A cargo net secures larger items. Both make your trunk usable and prevent items from becoming projectiles in a sudden stop.
