What to Look For in a Dash Cam

  • Resolution: At least 1440p front; 4K is ideal for reading license plates
  • Night vision: Look for "Starvis" or "Starlight" sensors
  • Storage: Get one that includes an SD card, or buy a high-endurance card
  • WiFi + app: Makes downloading clips much easier
  • Parking mode: Records even when your car is off (needs hardwire kit)
