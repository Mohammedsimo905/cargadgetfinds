Quick Buying Tips

  • Resolution: At least 1440p front; 4K is ideal for reading license plates.
  • Night vision: Look for "Starvis" or "Starlight" sensors for clear night footage.
  • Storage: Get one that includes an SD card, or buy a high-endurance card.
  • WiFi + app: Makes downloading clips to your phone much easier.
  • Parking mode: Records when your car is off — needs a hardwire kit.
